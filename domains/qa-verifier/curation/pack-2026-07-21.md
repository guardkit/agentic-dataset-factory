**Date:** 2026-07-21  |  **What this is:** the raw material for one sitting where you assign an outcome to each of the 79 autobuild bundles the census found with no committed verdict on disk.

## The one-minute version

Each entry below is one finished autobuild task: the coach looked at the evidence and reached a verdict, and the code went somewhere. Your job for each is one word:

- **approve** — the coach's verdict HELD. It said the work was sound and, as far as you can tell, it was. (A clean approve, blemishes and all.)
- **reject** — a real defect ESCAPED the coach. It approved something that was actually broken. If you pick reject, also give the **DC-class** (the kind of defect — table below) and, if you can, a one-line locus (where the defect is).
- **skip** — you're not sure, or the record doesn't let you decide cleanly. **Skip is always legitimate** and costs nothing; a guessed label is worse than a skip. Undecidable bundles are meant to be skipped.

You write your answers in the companion file `answers-2026-07-21.yaml` — same order as this pack, one entry per bundle.

### The DC-classes (only needed when you pick reject)

| Class | The kind of defect |
|---|---|
| DC-03 | composition seam — a call site drifted, a runner ships without its producer, or a soft-fail swallows the error (dead code) |
| DC-05 | environment tamper — the build changed its environment to pass |
| DC-08 | BDD holes — an undefined or unimplemented step was approved as passing |
| DC-12 | feature-plan defect — the plan itself is wrong in a bundle-visible way |
| DC-14 | direct-mode false-green — the narrative claims success the evidence doesn't support |

### How to read each entry

Every entry is at most six lines: the task, the date it was built, what the coach concluded, the blemishes it carried, and any **hint** I could dig out of the estate's own committed records (later fix commits, merges, retros, gate outcomes).

**About the hints:** a hint is *unverified evidence*, never a proposed answer. "A later commit fixed this task" is a lean toward reject; "merged clean and never touched again" is a lean toward approve — but the label is yours. Where I found nothing task-specific, the hint is just the group's merge record. Hints are marked **hint, unverified** every time.

### The order

Bundles are grouped by repo and feature. Groups run **easiest first**: clear-cut clusters (clean merges, or an obvious later fix) up top, murkier ones (mixed signals, thin evidence) toward the end. You can stop any time and resume — the answers file holds your place.
